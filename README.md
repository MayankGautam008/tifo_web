📖 About

TIFO (The Right Food. Right Person. Right Time.) is building an AI-powered personalized food ecosystem designed to transform how students, working professionals, and everyday consumers discover, subscribe to, and experience meals.

Unlike conventional food delivery platforms that primarily focus on restaurant discovery and one-time ordering, TIFO focuses on personalization, recurring meal experiences, AI-driven recommendations, and empowering local restaurant partners.

This repository contains the official corporate website representing TIFO's vision, mission, technology, validation, leadership, and future roadmap.

---

🌍 Vision

To build India's most intelligent and personalized food ecosystem where technology enables every individual to access affordable, convenient, and personalized meals based on their lifestyle and preferences.

---

🎯 Mission

To revolutionize everyday food experiences through personalization, artificial intelligence, recurring meal solutions, and strong local restaurant partnerships while making quality food more accessible and reliable.
